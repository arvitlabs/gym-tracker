# Gym Tracker — Exercise Library (Final v1)

Oct 18, 2026 · Module 5 seed content

Two separate parts:
- **Part A, Bodyweight:** baseline skills every user works toward (push, pull, squat, core + support).
- **Part B, Gym:** exercises per muscle group at two levels, beginner and intermediate.

Scheduling (which day, how many days) is decided later. This doc covers **what to do and how to do it.**

---

## Shared rules (apply to everything)

| Rule | Beginner | Intermediate |
|---|---|---|
| Sets per exercise | 2–3 | 3 |
| Reps: big lifts | 10–12 | 6–12 |
| Reps: small muscles (arms, raises, calves, abs) | 10–15 | 10–20 |
| Effort | Stop with 2–3 good reps left | Stop with 0–2 reps left (failure only on machines and isolation exercises) |
| Rest | 2 min big lifts, 1–1.5 min small | 2–3 min big lifts, 1–2 min small |
| Tempo | Lift with control, lower in 2–3 s | Same |
| Sets per muscle in one session | 6–9 | 6–9 |
| Intensity techniques | None | Half reps at the stretch, last set only, isolation and machines |

**Double progression** (all gym exercises):
1. Use the same weight until **every set hits the top of the rep range**.
2. Then add the smallest weight step and drop back to the bottom of the range.

**Warm-up:** before the first big lift, do light ×10 then medium ×5.

**Form rules:**
1. Control the lowering. No dropping or bouncing.
2. Full range, with a deep stretch at the bottom.
3. Stable body: no swinging, no momentum.
4. Brace the core on big lifts.
5. Feel the target muscle. If another muscle takes over, lower the weight.
6. Muscle burn is fine; joint pain is not. Change the angle, grip or exercise.
7. When form breaks, the set ends.

## Push-up

**Ladder:**

| Level | Variation | Move up when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wall push-up | 3×12 clean |
| 2 | Incline, hands at chest height (counter) | 3×12 clean |
| 3 | Incline, hands at hip height (desk) | 3×12 clean |
| 4 | Negatives: lower like a full push-up, come up on knees | 3×8 slow (3 s down) |
| 5 | Full push-up | 10 in one set (minimum) → 20 (goal) |
| Stretch | Decline → diamond → archer | — |

Knee push-ups are an optional side step only; incline push-ups carry over better.

**How to do it:**
1. Hands slightly wider than the shoulders.
2. Straight line from head to heels.
3. Lower until the chest is just above the floor, elbows at about 45° to the body.
4. Push back up to straight arms.

**Avoid:**
- Sagging or piked hips.
- Half reps.
- Elbows flared out to 90°.
- **Wrist pain:** turn the hands out slightly or use fists.

## Pull-up

**Ladder** (retest your max every month):

| Your max | What to train |
|---|---|
| Can't hang 30 s | Dead hang, building to 30–60 s |
| 0 reps | Scapular pulls 3×8–10 (2 s pause) + inverted rows 3×8–12 (steep → horizontal) |
| 0–4 reps | Inverted rows + negatives 3×3–5 (jump to the top, hold 5 s, lower over 5 s) |
| 5–7 reps | Sets of 2–5, always 2+ reps short of failure, 30–90 s rest, until 20 total reps (build to 30) + negatives |
| 8–9 reps | Same, with harder sets |
| 10+ | Goal reached |

**How to do it:**
1. Hands just wider than the shoulders, palms facing away.
2. Start from a full hang with arms straight.
3. Pull the shoulders down, then drive the elbows toward the hips until the chin clears the bar.
4. Lower all the way under control.

**Avoid:**
- Swinging or kipping.
- Half reps that stop short of a full hang.
- Doing a max-effort set every session.

**Setup note:** inverted rows need a low bar or a sturdy table.

## Squat

**Ladder:**

| Level | Variation | Move up when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chair squat (sit down to touch, stand up) | 3×15 |
| 2 | Bodyweight squat | 3×20 |
| 3 | Pause squat (2 s at the bottom) | 3×15 |
| 4 | Split squat | 3×10 per leg |
| 5 | Bulgarian split squat (rear foot on a chair) | 3×12 per leg |
| Stretch | Box pistol → pistol | — |

**How to do it:**
1. Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out.
2. Sit the hips back and down; knees track over the toes.
3. Go to at least thigh-parallel, chest up.
4. Drive up through the whole foot.

**Avoid:**
- Heels lifting. That signals tight ankles; see the ankle stretch in the mobility doc.
- Knees caving in.
- Shallow reps.

## Plank

**Ladder:** knee plank → full plank 20 s → 45 s → 60 s → long-lever plank (arms further forward) → rocking plank.

**How to do it:**
1. Forearms under the shoulders, body in a straight line.
2. Squeeze the glutes and abs hard.
3. Breathe normally.

**Avoid:** sagging or piked hips, holding your breath. Past 60 s, make it harder rather than longer.

## Support exercises

| Exercise | Progression | How to do it | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glute bridge | Bridge → 2 s pause at the top → single-leg | On your back, knees bent, feet flat; drive the hips up by squeezing the glutes until knees, hips and shoulders line up | Arching the lower back to get higher |
| Side plank | Knees → full → top-leg lift | Elbow under the shoulder, body straight, hips up | Hips sagging or rolling forward |
| Superman | Hold → alternating arm/leg → longer holds | Face down; lift the chest, arms and legs a few cm using the back and glutes | Cranking the neck up; keep your gaze on the floor |
| Calf raise | Both legs → single leg → on a step edge | Rise onto the balls of the feet, pause at the top, lower fully | Bouncing; short range |

## 6 ways to improve faster

1. **Harder variant:** the main engine. Hit the target, then move up a level.
2. **Grease the groove:** easy sets at about 50% of your max, spread through the day or week, never to failure.
3. **Negatives:** slow lowering trains a movement before you can complete it.
4. **Isometric holds** at the hardest point.
5. **Tempo and pauses:** 3 s down, 1 s pause.
6. **Weak-link accessories:**
   - Planks for push-ups.
   - Scapular pulls and dead hangs for pull-ups.
   - Glute bridges for squats.

# Part B — Gym

## B1. Beginner level

Machines first, simple dumbbell moves, no intensity techniques.

### Chest
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Machine chest press | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| Flat dumbbell press | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| Pec deck / machine fly | 2 × 12–15 | 1.5 min |

- **Machine chest press:**
  - Set the seat so the handles line up with mid-chest.
  - Shoulder blades back and down; press without locking the elbows hard.
  - Return slowly until you feel a chest stretch.
- **Flat dumbbell press:**
  - Lie flat with feet planted and shoulder blades squeezed together.
  - Lower the dumbbells to chest level, elbows at about 45°, then press up.
  - *Avoid:* bouncing at the bottom, flaring the elbows to 90°.
- **Pec deck:**
  - Arms slightly bent and set at chest height.
  - Bring the hands together while the chest stays up.
  - Open slowly into a stretch.
  - *Avoid:* shoulders rolling forward.

### Back
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Lat pulldown, wide grip | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| Seated cable row | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| Lat pulldown, close / neutral grip | 2 × 10–12 | 2 min |

- **Lat pulldown:**
  - Thighs locked under the pad, lean back slightly.
  - Pull the bar to the upper chest by driving the elbows down toward the hips.
  - Let the arms go fully straight at the top.
  - *Avoid:* yanking with your bodyweight, pulling behind the neck.
- **Seated cable row:**
  - Chest up, slight knee bend.
  - Pull the handle to the stomach, squeezing the shoulder blades together.
  - Let them open fully on the way back.
  - *Avoid:* rocking the torso, shrugging.

### Legs
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Leg press (or goblet squat) | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| Leg curl | 3 × 12–15 | 1.5 min |
| Light dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 2 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| Hip thrust | 3 × 10–15 | 1.5 min |
| Calf raise machine | 3 × 12–15 | 1 min |

- **Leg press:**
  - Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform.
  - Lower until the knees are at about 90° or deeper, as long as the lower back stays on the pad.
  - Push through the whole foot.
  - *Avoid:* locking the knees out hard, letting the hips lift off the seat.
- **Goblet squat:**
  - Hold a dumbbell at the chest; squat between the knees with the chest up.
  - Same cues as the bodyweight squat.
- **Leg curl:**
  - Pad just above the heels.
  - Curl fully, lower slowly to almost straight.
  - *Avoid:* hips lifting off the pad.
- **Dumbbell Romanian deadlift:**
  - Dumbbells in front of the thighs, soft knees.
  - Push the hips back while the dumbbells slide down the legs, back flat, until you feel a hamstring stretch (about mid-shin).
  - Stand up by driving the hips forward.
  - *Avoid:* rounding the back, turning it into a squat.
- **Hip thrust:**
  - Upper back on a bench, feet flat; at the top, knees should be over the feet.
  - Chin tucked; drive the hips up and squeeze the glutes for 1 s.
  - *Avoid:* arching the lower back at the top.
- **Calf raise machine:**
  - Pause 1–2 s in the bottom stretch, rise as high as possible, lower slowly.
  - *Avoid:* bouncing.

### Shoulders
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Machine shoulder press | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| Dumbbell lateral raise | 3 × 12–15 | 1 min |
| Reverse pec deck | 3 × 12–15 | 1 min |

- **Machine shoulder press:**
  - Handles start at about chin level, back against the pad.
  - Press overhead without arching the back.
- **Dumbbell lateral raise:**
  - Slight elbow bend; raise the arms out to the sides up to shoulder height, leading with the elbows.
  - Lower slowly.
  - *Avoid:* swinging, shrugging, going heavy.
- **Reverse pec deck:**
  - Face the pad, arms straight ahead.
  - Sweep the arms back and out to shoulder level.
  - *Avoid:* squeezing the shoulder blades hard; let the rear shoulders do the work.

### Arms
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell curl | 3 × 10–12 | 1.5 min |
| Cable pushdown | 3 × 10–12 | 1.5 min |
| Hammer curl | 2 × 12–15 | 1 min |
| Overhead dumbbell extension | 2 × 12–15 | 1 min |

- **Dumbbell curl:**
  - Elbows pinned to the sides.
  - Curl up while turning the palms up; lower fully.
  - *Avoid:* swinging the body, elbows drifting forward.
- **Cable pushdown:**
  - Elbows pinned at the sides; push down to straight arms.
  - Let the forearms come up to about 90° on the return.
- **Hammer curl:** same as the curl with the palms facing each other. Easier on the elbows and trains the forearms.
- **Overhead dumbbell extension:**
  - Hold one dumbbell overhead with both hands.
  - Lower it behind the head with elbows pointing up, then extend.
  - *Avoid:* elbows flaring wide, arching the back.

### Abs
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Crunch | 3 × 12–15 | 1 min |
| Reverse crunch (knees tucked) | 3 × 10–15 | 1 min |

- **Crunch:**
  - Curl the ribcage toward the pelvis ("C" shape) until the shoulder blades just leave the floor.
  - *Avoid:* pulling on the neck, sitting all the way up (that's the hip flexors).
- **Reverse crunch:**
  - Knees tucked; curl the hips up off the floor toward the chest.
  - Lower slowly.
  - *Avoid:* swinging the legs.

## B2. Intermediate level

Free-weight compounds, angle tweaks, and a stretch focus.
- **Allowed:** half reps at the stretch on the last set of isolation and machine exercises.
- **Never:** half reps or failure on heavy squats or deadlifts.

### Chest
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | How to do it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incline dumbbell press | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 min | Bench 1–2 notches up (up to 30–45° if the chest is steep). Lighter weight, deep stretch at the bottom, elbows at about 45° |
| Barbell bench press | 3 × 4–6 | 3 min | Shoulder blades pinned back, feet driving into the floor. Bar touches the lower chest, press up and slightly back. Use a spotter or safety bars |
| Seated cable fly | 3 × 10–15 | 1.5 min | Cables at shoulder height, pad behind the back for a deeper stretch. Squeeze the biceps toward the chest; stop the set when the shoulders roll forward |

### Back
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | How to do it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral-grip pulldown, leaning back | 3 × 10–15 | 2 min | Lean back 10–15°, elbows in front, drive them toward the hips while pushing the shoulders down |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min | Elbow tight to the side, sweeping in an arc toward the hip. Forearm vertical; don't twist the torso |
| Chest-supported row, bench about 30° | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min | Elbows at about 45° (arrow shape). Let the shoulder blades open fully, then squeeze them together |
| Chest-supported row, bench about 60° | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min | Elbows flared, row up and back with a shrug for the upper traps |

### Shoulders
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | How to do it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seated dumbbell shoulder press | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min | Bench 1–2 notches below upright. Elbows flare slightly on the press, tuck on the way down |
| Cable lateral raise | 3 × 10–20 | 1 min | Cable slightly above the floor, arm starts behind the body. Add weight once all sets reach 15 |
| Reverse cable fly | 3 × 10–15 | 1 min | Cables slightly above the shoulders, cross the arms to grab the opposite handles, sweep out wide |

### Arms
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | How to do it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lying incline dumbbell curl | 3 × 8–12 | 1.5 min | Arms hang behind the body; curl without the elbows moving forward. Hardest at the bottom stretch |
| Reverse preacher curl or behind-the-body cable curl | 3 × 10–15 | 1.5 min | Alternatives with the same stretch focus |
| Overhead cable or rope extension | 3 × 10–15 | 1.5 min | Face away from the cable, elbows overhead; extend fully, stretch deep. Pick the angle that's pain-free for the elbows |

### Legs
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | How to do it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbell squat | 3 × 6–8 | 3 min | Bar on the upper back, brace, sit down to at least parallel, knees over toes. Elevate the heels if depth is hard |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 6–8 | 3 min | Same hinge as the dumbbell version; bar stays touching the legs, stop when the hamstrings are fully stretched |
| Walking lunge | 3 × 6–10 per leg | 2 min | Long step, back knee almost touching the floor, slight forward lean for the glutes |
| Leg extension | 3 × 10–15 | 1.5 min | Lean back if the machine allows (more stretch on the front thigh); full lockout, slow lowering |
| Seated leg curl | 3 × 10–15 | 1.5 min | Preferred over lying: more hamstring stretch. Full range |
| Barbell hip thrust | 3 × 10–15 | 2 min | Same cues as beginner, with a barbell and a pad |
| Glute-dominant step-down | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min | Stand on a bench at thigh-parallel height, weight in the opposite hand. Lower the free leg slowly, knee not drifting far forward |
| Standing calf raise | 3 × 10–15 | 1 min | Straight legs (standing beats seated for growth); 1–2 s pause in the bottom stretch |

### Abs
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | How to do it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reverse crunch, legs straighter | 3 × 6–12 | 1 min | Straighter legs make it harder; curl the spine (C shape), never swing |
| Rope cable crunch | 3 × 8–15 | 1 min | Hips locked at 90°, rope by the ears; fold the chest toward the pelvis. Add weight at 3×12 |
