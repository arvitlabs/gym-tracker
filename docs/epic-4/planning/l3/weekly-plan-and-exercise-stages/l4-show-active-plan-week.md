# L4 — Show the active plan week

**ID** GT-03.01\
**Parent** [L3 — Weekly plan and exercise stages](l3-weekly-plan-and-exercise-stages.md)\
**Board issue type** Task

**Scope** Show training and rest days, day focus, and ordered sections and exercises. Link exercises to their detail and stage screens.

**Depends on** [First plan](../onboarding-and-first-plan/l3-onboarding-and-first-plan.md) and an authenticated active-plan read.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given the user has a four- or five-day active plan, when they open Plan · Week and a training day, then they see the assigned week and that day's ordered sections and exercises.
- Given a day has no training assignment, when the week is shown, then that day appears as a rest day.
