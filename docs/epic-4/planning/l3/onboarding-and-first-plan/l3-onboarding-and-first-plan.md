# L3 — Onboarding and first plan

**ID** GT-02\
**Parent** [L2 — Phase 1 MVP](../../l2/phase-1-mvp.md)\
**Board issue type** Epic

**Outcome** A newly signed-in user enters their preferences and gets an editable four- or five-day plan for gym or home.

**Done when** A tester can finish onboarding and see their first plan in under three minutes from install. The plan matches their experience, location, and schedule. The result screen links to Today and Plan · Week.

**Scope** Screens 4–8, the Phase 1 exercises and templates needed for generation, and saving the user's first active plan.

## L4 tasks in dependency order

1. [Capture Body & Goal](l4-capture-body-and-goal.md)
2. [Capture experience, location, and schedule](l4-capture-training-preferences.md)
3. [Capture optional lifestyle and body details](l4-capture-optional-lifestyle.md)
4. [Show the onboarding review](l4-review-onboarding-answers.md)
5. [Seed Phase 1 exercises and plan templates](l4-seed-plan-templates.md)
6. [Generate and save the first plan](l4-generate-first-plan.md)
7. [Show the generated plan](l4-show-plan-result.md)

**Proposed first slice** Complete the flow with one approved plan template and verify that it saves and can be opened again. Cover all approved combinations before closing the Epic. Full generation depends on a runnable database, the first migration, and versioned seeds. Sprint sizing and selection are pending.
