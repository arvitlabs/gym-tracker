# L4 — Capture Body & Goal

**ID** GT-02.01\
**Parent** [L3 — Onboarding and first plan](l3-onboarding-and-first-plan.md)\
**Board issue type** Task

**Scope** Collect height, weight, age, gender, and one of the two Phase 1 goals. Store the goal for the profile.

**Depends on** [Account access](../account-access/l3-account-access.md) and the onboarding route.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a signed-in user enters valid values and selects Build muscle or General fitness, when they continue, then the answers carry forward to the next step.
- Given a required field is missing or invalid, when the user continues, then they see the relevant field error and stay on the step.
