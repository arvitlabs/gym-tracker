# L2 — Phase 1 MVP

**Parent** [L1 — Gym Tracker](../l1/gym-tracker.md)

Deliver the core workout loop as an installable web app for two to three private testers.

- Sign in and generate a four- or five-day gym or home plan.
- Follow today's workout with warm-up and stretch sections, exercise instructions, and manual stage progression.
- Log workouts offline and sync when back online.
- View workout history and progress toward weekly and monthly workout-day goals.

**Done when** A tester can install the app, generate their first plan in under three minutes, log a full workout offline, and see the saved workout and updated goal progress after reconnecting.
