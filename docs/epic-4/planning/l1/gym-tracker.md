# L1 — Gym Tracker

Help beginner and intermediate gym-goers train consistently by giving them a weekly plan they can follow, customize, and log.

Gym Tracker brings workout planning, warm-ups and stretches, workout logging, and progress together. Its first milestone is the [Phase 1 MVP](../l2/phase-1-mvp.md).

**Success measure** A tester logs at least three planned workouts per week for three consecutive weeks without reminders from the team.
